TECHSHOP CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

Company:
TechShop is an online electronics store that sells laptops,
smartphones, headphones, tablets, and other electronic products.

ORDER AND DELIVERY:

1. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days.

2. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days.

3. Customers can check their order status using their order ID.

4. Delivery is available across India.

RETURNS:

5. Customers can return eligible products within 7 days of delivery.

6. Products must be unused and should have the original packaging.

7. Some products may not be eligible for return.

REFUNDS:

8. Refunds are normally processed within 5 to 7 business days after
the returned product has been inspected.

9. The refund is sent to the original payment method.

DAMAGED PRODUCTS:

10. If a product arrives damaged, the customer should contact
customer support within 48 hours.

11. Customers may be eligible for a replacement or refund
after verification.

PAYMENTS:

12. TechShop accepts credit cards, debit cards, UPI and
Cash on Delivery.

13. Cash on Delivery is available for orders below ₹10,000.

SUPPORT:

14. Customers should provide their order ID when asking about
a specific order.

15. If the AI cannot answer a question using the available
company information, it should tell the customer that the issue
needs to be handled by human support.
